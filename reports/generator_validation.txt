ACF REGRESSION ATTRIBUTION: is the periodic reset the cause?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BNB and MATIC's acf_abs_lag1 regressed from PASS (v1.5) to WARN (v2). Tested by
rerunning each coin's idio channel WITH vs WITHOUT the reset (reset_period > n_hours)
across 20 seeds (50000h each) -- a single seed turned out to be too
noisy to trust for a near-unit-root process (see std_diff below).
coin     mean_acf_with  mean_acf_without  mean_reset_impact  std_diff
BTC             0.3936            0.4140            -0.0204    0.0428
ETH             0.2433            0.2529            -0.0096    0.0109
BNB             0.4014            0.4133            -0.0119    0.0327
MATIC           0.3173            0.3544            -0.0372    0.0347
SOL             0.2999            0.3353            -0.0353    0.0350

Largest mean reset impact on idio's own acf_abs_lag1: 0.0372 (MATIC/SOL)
-- real and systematic (all 5 coins negative), but well under half of the ~0.10
shortfall seen at the coin level, and the per-seed std (~0.03-0.04) shows single-path
estimates of this effect are NOT reliable on their own (an earlier single-seed check
in this development gave a misleadingly large estimate for exactly this reason --
flagging that here rather than silently using the better number).

So the reset is a real but MINOR contributor. Bigger cause: v1.5's idio variance had
collapsed (see ann_vol[BNB] WARN in the prior report), so BNB/MATIC's total return was
dominated by beta*market_factor -- whose own acf_abs_lag1 (~0.31) propped up the
coin-level acf, coincidentally close to real. v2 restores idio's correct variance
share (ann_vol back to PASS, see above) -- but mixing two INDEPENDENTLY-timed
clustering processes (factor + idio) mechanically dilutes the combined series's
lag-1 |return| autocorrelation below either component's own acf (same aggregation-
dilution mechanism flagged for kurtosis in the very first v1 report, now showing up
again for acf on the two highest idio-share coins). Not tuned away, per instructions --
reported as-is. A secondary, separate finding: the wide per-seed std above means
different seeds can realize meaningfully different vol-clustering strength for these
near-unit-root coins -- worth knowing before relying on acf-sensitive fitness signals
from a single generated world.
